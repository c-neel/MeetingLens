BACKGROUND & CONTEXT
Problem Statement: The Meeting Productivity Crisis
1. Information Loss & Fatigue
  • Lengthy 30–60+ minute meetings overwhelm human working memory.
  • Critical context, decisions, and technical reasoning are lost after calls end.
  • Recordings are rarely rewatched due to lack of time and indexing.
2. Manual Note-Taking Flaws
  • Designated note-takers cannot actively participate in brainstorming.
  • Human notes are subjective, prone to personal bias, and incomplete.
  • Inconsistent note formats prevent team-wide searchability.
3. Task Accountability Void
  • Over 40% of assigned tasks fall through the cracks post-meeting.
  • Verbal task assignments lack clear deadlines and ownership tags.
  • No built-in verification mechanism to review and confirm tasks.

SOLUTION OVERVIEW
Expected Outcome & Proposed Solution: MeetingLens
Core Proposed Solution
  • Multi-Modal Capture: Record live audio with real-time Speech-to-Text or upload audio, video, and text logs.
  • Multi-Tier AI Summaries: Executive high-level takeaways, chronological topic breakdowns, and decision registries.
  • Automated Task Extraction: LLM detects actionable tasks, assignees, deadlines, and urgency tags.
Key Deliverables & Governance
  • Human-in-the-Loop Task Approval: Interactive verification modal to review, edit, or reassign AI tasks before saving.
  • Meeting Quality Scoring: Objective metric evaluating meeting clarity, efficiency, and actionable outcomes (0–100%).
  • Searchable Archive & Reports: Instant historical query engine with downloadable PDF/Markdown reports.

ALGORITHM & LOGIC
Algorithm: Meeting Intelligence Pipeline (MIP)
Algorithm: MeetingIntelligencePipeline(InputSource, CurrentUser)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. IF InputSource is LiveAudio THEN Transcript = WebSpeechTranscribe(InputSource)
   ELSE Transcript = SanitizeAndExtractText(InputSource)
2. CleanText = RemoveNoiseAndFillerWords(Transcript)
3. PromptPayload = BuildStructuredPrompt(Role="Executive PM", Transcript=CleanText, Schema="JSON")
4. AI_Response = InvokeLLM_API(PromptPayload)
5. ParsedData = ValidateAndParseJSON(AI_Response)
6. DB_Transaction_Begin():
     MeetingID = InsertMeeting(user_id=CurrentUser.id, title=ParsedData.title, summary=ParsedData.summary,
                               executive_summary=ParsedData.executive_summary, quality_score=ParsedData.quality_score)
     FOR EACH task IN ParsedData.action_items:
         InsertActionItem(meeting_id=MeetingID, description=task.desc, assignee=task.owner, deadline=task.due)
   DB_Transaction_Commit()
7. Trigger TaskApprovalModal(MeetingID, ActionItems) for Human Verification & Editing
8. Return Structured Meeting Dashboard View

RISK MANAGEMENT
Assumptions, Challenges & Mitigation Strategies
Assumptions
  • Audio Hardware & Network: Stable microphone input and active network connection for LLM processing.
  • Speaker Attribution: Participants state names during speech or contextual cues allow owner extraction.
  • Language Baseline: Primary spoken language is English with support for technical jargon.
Challenges & Mitigations
  • LLM Hallucinations: Enforce strict JSON schemas and multi-pattern regex sanitization fallbacks.
  • Broken / Noisy Transcripts: Dual-tier cleaning (client preprocessing + LLM context repair).
  • Ambiguous Deadlines & Tasks: Human-in-the-loop Task Approval screen prevents false assignments.
